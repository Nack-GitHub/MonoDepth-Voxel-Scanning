\section{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Dataset and reference}
ARKitScenes~\cite{arkitscenes} is the only dataset that provides, for the same room, RGB video from a real device
(iPad~Pro, $640\times480$), that device's LiDAR depth ($256\times192$) and Faro laser depth registered to the camera
poses ($1920\times1440$, $\sim$4~fps). \textbf{Ground truth is the Faro data as distributed.} In 2D we compare per-frame
depth with Faro depth directly (nearest-neighbour down-sampling, so every GT value is a measured one). In 3D, since no
laser mesh is shipped, we fuse Faro depth from every frame at \SI{1}{cm} voxels once per scene into a reference mesh.
The \code{gt} row at \SI{4}{cm} therefore has non-zero error---the pipeline's own discretisation loss---and because
the reference uses the same poses as every row, pose drift is excluded and all differences are due to the depth source.
iPad LiDAR is never used as ground truth.

\subsection{Fine-tuning protocol}\label{sec:ftsetup}
Scenes disjoint from the test set at the \code{visit\_id} level were drawn with seed~0: 10 training scenes with both
Faro and LiDAR, 14 more with LiDAR only, and 3 Validation-fold scenes for checkpoint selection. Three fine-tunes share
one recipe: \textbf{R1} uses Faro labels on the 10 scenes (\num{3594} frames), \textbf{R2} iPad LiDAR (confidence
$\geq 1$) on the same 10 scenes (\num{8656} frames), and \textbf{R3, the main model}, LiDAR on all 24 scenes
(\num{19896} frames). R1 and R2 do not share frames: Faro exists at $\sim$4~fps, LiDAR with every colour frame (we take
every third, $\sim$10~fps), so R2 sees $\sim$2.4$\times$ more distinct views than R1. The DINOv2 encoder is frozen;
only the DPT neck and metric head train, with SiLog loss, AdamW (lr \num{5e-5}, cosine schedule), batch 8 (2 $\times$
4 accumulation) on $518\times518$ crops, for \num{6000} steps (R1, R2) or \num{12000} (R3), keeping the checkpoint with
the lowest validation AbsRel. A \num{6000}-step run takes about an hour on an RTX~3070. At test time the fine-tuned
models are plain \code{DepthSource} rows: RGB in, metres out, \code{identity} aligner, same input preparation as the
pretrained model.

\subsection{Metrics and experiments}\label{sec:metrics}
\textbf{3D:} 200k points sampled on both meshes; accuracy $=$ mean pred$\rightarrow$ref distance, completeness $=$
ref$\rightarrow$pred, Chamfer $=$ their mean; precision/recall/F-score at $\tau=2/5/10$~cm, with \fscore{} as
headline (\SI{5}{cm} $\approx$ furniture-placement tolerance). \textbf{2D:} per-frame AbsRel, $\delta_1$ and RMSE
against Faro depth; pixels are selected by the ground truth (\SIrange{0.1}{5}{m}) and predictions clipped to that
range, averaged per room then over rooms. Table~\ref{tab:exps} lists the experiments; numbers are mean $\pm$ std over
the six scenes. \textbf{Exp.~7} adds \rsVN{} Validation-fold rooms (seed~0, one video per visit, excluding every visit
of the training, validation and test sets) evaluated in 2D only on every second Faro frame; these rooms decided nothing.
